This policy explains how HealthMe for iPhone and Apple Watch, developed by Andrei Constantinescu, handles information. For privacy questions, contact andrei.cristian.costa@gmail.com.

1. No account, advertising or tracking

HealthMe does not require an account and has no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking SDKs. The app does not send your health records or scores to the developer or to a developer-operated server. Health analysis happens on your device.

2. Apple Health access

With your permission, HealthMe reads existing records through Apple HealthKit to display your measurements, history and wellness estimates. Depending on availability and the access you grant, these include heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and sleep stages, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, sleeping wrist temperature, breathing disturbances, sleep apnea events, active and resting calories, steps, walking and running distance, exercise minutes, recorded workouts, and high, low or irregular heart rate events. The app also uses record dates and source information to organize and identify the data.

HealthMe requests read access only. It does not write to, modify or delete your Apple Health records. It does not start workouts, GPS tracking or continuous sensor measurements. You can manage access in Apple Health or system settings. An empty result does not tell the app whether data is missing or access has been denied.

3. Local storage and retention

HealthMe stores local copies of relevant records, synchronization information, calculated results and history so it can display and update your dashboard. Appearance, language, app icon and other preferences are also stored locally.

Imported source records use a rolling retention window. Saved score history, including Stress history, can remain longer than that window until you delete HealthMe data or the app invalidates affected results following source changes. A normal refresh does not clear that history. HealthMe is not an archival backup of Apple Health.

The health database uses system file protection on iPhone and Apple Watch and is configured to be excluded from device backup. Apple Health synchronization, system backups and diagnostics are managed separately by Apple under your settings and Apple's policies. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. iPhone, Apple Watch and complications

After you explicitly associate your devices inside HealthMe, dated score summaries and the control information needed for that association can pass between your iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity service. HealthMe does not send the original health records through this score-sharing channel. Apple may independently synchronize Health data between your devices.

The app and its complication extension share limited display data locally on the same device. Watch faces may show health-related values when you add HealthMe complications. You control which complications you display. Apple controls their refresh timing, so an old value may remain visible until the system refreshes it.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can change Health permissions and use Settings → Delete HealthMe data to remove HealthMe's local health-data copy and stop analysis on that device. This does not delete your original Apple Health records. Analysis can be resumed explicitly in the app.

HealthMe also requests removal of previously shared scores from the associated device. That removal remains unconfirmed until the other device responds. Stop its own local analysis separately if you want analysis stopped on both devices. Removing or refreshing complications may be necessary to clear previously rendered values. The developer cannot remotely access or recover your local health database.

6. Support correspondence

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The email provider processes that correspondence to deliver and store it. It is used to answer your request, investigate problems and follow up, and kept as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations.

Opening a Support or Privacy link does not automatically attach or send health records. Please do not email Apple Health exports, passwords or sensitive health screenshots. You can contact the same support address to request deletion of correspondence, subject to any required retention.

7. Public website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no added analytics, tracking scripts, advertising, remote fonts or embedded third-party media. Opening them sends a normal website request to GitHub, which may process technical request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is separate from the app's local health analysis.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when features or data practices change. The date above identifies this revision. Contact Andrei Constantinescu at andrei.cristian.costa@gmail.com for privacy questions or support.
