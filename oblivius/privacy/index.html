Privacy Policy

How Oblivius handles information on your device and when you contact support.

Support · Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy applies to Oblivius for iPhone and iPad, developed by Andrei Constantinescu. For questions, email andrei.cristian.costa@gmail.com.

1. No account or tracking

Oblivius does not require an account and has no advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking. The app does not send your reminders or usage activity to the developer and does not operate a cloud synchronization service.

2. Information on your device

The app stores reminders, titles, notes, schedules, completion history, settings, sound profiles and imported audio locally. It also keeps local recovery copies that can contain earlier reminders, settings and sounds. Preferences and derived alert information are stored on the device.

Device backups and other services you enable in iOS are managed separately by Apple. The app uses iOS file protection for its local files; this is not a claim that the reminder database is separately encrypted.

3. Notifications, alarms and widgets

Oblivius schedules local notifications and, where available and permitted, alarms using iOS services. Notification preview settings control whether reminder text is shown. App Lock alone does not hide notification text. Notification and alarm presentation is controlled by the system and your settings.

The app shares a limited local snapshot with its widget extension through an App Group on the same device. Protected reminder content is redacted before the snapshot is written. Widgets can show reminder information on your Home Screen; iOS controls their refresh. You can remove a widget through iOS.

4. App Lock and imported sounds

If you enable App Lock, iOS handles authentication using available device authentication such as Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. Oblivius does not receive or store your facial or fingerprint data.

When you choose an audio file to import, the app copies and converts it into app storage. The original source path is not stored. Imported sounds and their metadata can be included in backups.

5. Backups and storage providers

You can export reminders, settings and sounds to a location you choose in Files. Password protection is optional. A password-protected export is encrypted; an unprotected export can be read or changed by anyone with the file. The app does not store your backup password and cannot recover it for you.

Your selected Files provider may store the export in the cloud under its own terms. Oblivius does not upload it to the developer. Local recovery copies stay on the device and can be rotated; they are not a substitute for a separate backup if the app is removed or the device is lost.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete reminders, change preferences, remove widgets and manage exported files. Delete All Data removes the active reminders and history, resets applicable settings, clears widget data and cancels alerts. It retains local recovery copies, including a copy created before deletion; the oldest copy may be rotated. Some device-local display and haptic preferences remain. Cleanup of old alerts or sounds can be retried if necessary.

Delete All does not remove files you exported to Files or copies in device backups. Manage those separately. Removing the app removes its local recovery copies; keep a verified separate backup before uninstalling if you may need your data.

7. Support correspondence

If you contact support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Email providers process the correspondence to deliver and store it. It is used to answer your request and investigate issues, and is kept as needed for those purposes and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of your correspondence using the same address, subject to required retention. Please do not send passwords or backup files.

8. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no added analytics, tracking scripts or advertising. GitHub may process technical information about website requests under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting the website is separate from using the app.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may change when features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Contact andrei.cristian.costa@gmail.com with privacy questions.
