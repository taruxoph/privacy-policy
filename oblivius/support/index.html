Create and manage reminders

On the calendar page, scroll to the top and pull the list down to create a reminder. Enter a title, choose a date and time, then save. Tap a reminder's text to edit it, its checkbox to complete it, or touch and hold it for more actions. Tap a completed reminder's checkbox to reopen it.

Choose All day for a date-only reminder without an alert. When reopening an all-day task, choose Today or Tomorrow. Repeating reminders have options for their schedule and completion.

Notifications and alarms

Allow notifications in iOS Settings and check your device's volume, Silent Mode and Focus if you do not hear an alert. For a banner that remains visible, choose Persistent under iOS Settings → Notifications → Oblivius → Banner Style. iOS controls delivery and presentation; alerts can be delayed or silenced.

Alarms require iOS 26 or later and permission. Alarm Stop silences the alarm without completing the reminder. Live Activities are needed for the alarm snooze countdown. Keep your device powered on. Do not rely on Oblivius for emergencies or life-critical reminders.

Widgets and privacy

Add an Oblivius widget from the Home Screen widget gallery. Open Settings → Appearance → Widgets to change its colors or show completed reminders. If a widget asks to refresh, open Oblivius. iOS controls widget updates, so changes may not appear immediately.

Settings → Privacy offers App Lock, notification preview and notification action controls. App Lock controls access to the app; it does not itself hide notification text. Choose your notification preview setting separately.

Back up and restore

Open Settings → Backup and tap Backup Now to save a file using Files. You can protect the export with a password of 4–12 characters. Keep the file outside the app and remember the password: a forgotten password cannot be recovered. An unprotected file can be read or changed by anyone who has it.

Choose Restore from File to review a backup before confirming replacement of your current reminders, settings and sounds. Local recovery copies remain on the device and are lost if the app is removed or the device is lost. Keep a separate usable backup before deleting the app.

Delete data

Delete All Data removes the active reminder data, history, sounds and resettable settings, and cancels alerts. It keeps local recovery copies, including a new copy made before deletion; older copies may be rotated. Files backups you saved elsewhere remain under your control. Some display and haptic preferences remain on the device.
