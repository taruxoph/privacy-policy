Sukala for macOS

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Sukala is a local-first battery and system monitor. Monitoring data remains on your Mac.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sukala macOS application handles information while displaying battery, power-adapter, CPU, memory, and temperature readings.

2. Data collection by the developer

Sukala does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your monitoring data, files, usage history, contacts, location, or device identifiers to the developer.

The current app does not operate a developer server and does not include analytics, advertising, telemetry, crash-reporting, cloud synchronization, licensing, or automatic-update services.

3. Information processed locally

To provide live monitoring, Sukala reads and processes the following information locally on your Mac:

  • battery charge, health, capacity, cycle, temperature, voltage, current, and charging-state readings;
  • power-source and power-adapter information;
  • aggregate CPU usage and used and total physical memory;
  • read-only CPU-temperature and adapter-input measurements when available.

Sukala does not use these features to enumerate your files, applications, users, or per-process activity. It does not request hardware serial numbers, platform UUIDs, network identifiers, or other unique remote identifiers.

4. Local storage and exports

Live readings and recent operational events may be held temporarily in memory while Sukala is running. The current app does not maintain a persistent monitoring-history database.

If you choose a manual JSON or CSV export in a version that exposes that feature, the file is saved only to the local location you select. Sukala does not upload exported files.

5. System Information

When you select the graphical battery, Sukala can open Apple’s System Information app at Hardware > Power. Sukala does not read, save, change, or transmit the System Information report.

6. Network, analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current Sukala app does not make network requests. It does not use analytics services, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, fingerprinting, or third-party profiling tools.

Sukala does not show ads and does not track your activity across apps, websites, or services.

7. Support and data sharing

Sukala does not automatically create or upload support reports. If you contact the developer by email, you decide what information to include, and your email service providers process that message under their own privacy terms.

Sukala does not sell, rent, or share personal data collected by the app because the app does not collect that data.

8. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Sukala does not retain your information on developer-operated servers, so there is no server-side account or personal-data record to delete. Temporary in-memory monitoring data is discarded when the app exits.

You control any files you explicitly export and may delete them from the selected location at any time.

9. Children’s privacy

Sukala does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Sukala’s features or data practices change. Any future network service, such as updates, licensing, or support links, will be described here when applicable. The effective date above identifies the current version.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact andrei.cristian.costa@gmail.com.

Sukala currently performs monitoring only. It does not modify battery charging or write to battery hardware.
