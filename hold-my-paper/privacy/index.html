Hold My Paper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Hold My Paper is designed as a local-first Mac app for private plain-text papers.

Local-first by design

Your papers are stored on your Mac. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, does not track you, does not show ads, and does not include third-party SDKs.

What the app stores

Hold My Paper stores your papers, paper names, Books, local history, recovery backups, preferences, themes, fonts, and app settings on your Mac.

If you choose to import, export, back up, or restore files, the app uses the files and folders you select through standard macOS file panels.

Network and tracking

Hold My Paper does not send your paper content to a server.

The app does not use advertising identifiers, tracking pixels, analytics services, crash-reporting SDKs, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Local automation

The optional quickpad:// feature is local and off by default. If you enable "Let Other Apps Add Text Here," other local apps can add text using supported local URLs.
