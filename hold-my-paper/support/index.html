Opening the app

Hold My Paper lives in the Mac menu bar. Click the pen-nib menu bar icon to open your papers, Browser, backup tools, preferences, and Quit command.

Importing and exporting text

Use the menu bar actions to import TXT files, export papers, export Books, or create an Export Package.

Backup and restore

Use Backup to ZIP to create a local backup. Use Restore from ZIP to restore a Hold My Paper backup. Restore validates the archive before replacing the current workspace.

History and Recovery

History and Recovery help you revisit older local snapshots and recover previous paper states.

Local automation

The quickpad:// feature is off by default. Enable "Let Other Apps Add Text Here" from the menu only if you want local apps to append or prepend text.

Privacy

Hold My Paper does not require an account and does not use analytics, tracking, ads, cloud sync, or third-party SDKs.
