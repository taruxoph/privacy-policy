Starting an awake session

Open Racophorus from the Mac menu bar. Choose System when tasks should continue while the display may rest, or Display and System when both the display and the Mac should remain awake. Then select a duration or start an indefinite session.

Choosing an exact end time

Open Settings and use Keep awake until to choose the exact date and time when sleep should be allowed again.

Ending a session

Select Allow Sleep from the Racophorus panel. This releases the active awake session and allows macOS to sleep normally again.

Shortcuts and quick access

You can configure the global shortcut in Settings. Racophorus also provides App Shortcuts for Keep Awake, Allow Sleep, and Check Status.

Battery warnings

Optional battery warnings can be enabled in Settings. Choose the warning threshold that works for you. A warning does not interrupt the active awake session.

If your Mac still sleeps

Confirm that Racophorus is running and that the panel shows Keeping Awake. System mode allows the display to rest, while Display and System mode keeps both active. Hardware and macOS safety behavior, including sleep caused by closing a MacBook lid, cannot be overridden by Racophorus.

Privacy

Racophorus does not require an account and does not use analytics, tracking, ads, or telemetry. Preferences remain on your Mac.
